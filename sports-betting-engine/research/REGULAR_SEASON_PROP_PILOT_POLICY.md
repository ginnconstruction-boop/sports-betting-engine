# Frozen regular-season prop pilot — December 7, 2025

Specification written before this pilot's archive or outcomes are retrieved. It is a small retrospective development diagnostic, not an independent preregistered holdout: earlier work already inspected some 2025 outcomes. Never optimize or rerun selections after viewing their results.

- Common decision cutoff: 2025-12-07 16:00:00 UTC (10 AM Central).
- Discover historical regular-season NFL events at that cutoff. Take the first two games starting December 7 UTC, at least 30 minutes after cutoff, sorted by kickoff then provider event ID. Do not substitute games because prices, history or results are missing.
- One historical event-odds call per game; US region, passing yards, rushing yards, receiving yards and receptions. Maximum archive budget 81 credits (1 discovery + 2 × 40). Cache raw requests locally without API keys; no paid retries or additional dates.
- FanDuel and BetMGM only. Include every uniquely named player/market quoted by these books, both sides and exact lines. Safety cap 60 unique player identities; fail rather than silently truncate. Missing quotes/players remain in the coverage report.
- Snapshot must be at/before cutoff and no older than 10 minutes. Individual quotes must be at/before cutoff and no older than 15 minutes. No future-timestamp tolerance for historical data.
- Resolve player IDs by exact normalized name on the two teams' ESPN 2025 rosters (season-specific core API athlete lists; the presentation roster endpoint returns empty older-season groups). This is identity lookup only: season rosters retrieved now do NOT prove pregame team membership, health, starting role or availability. Missing/ambiguous identities are excluded, never guessed from final-game participants.
- Retrieve 2023–2025 logs; only completed regular-season observations strictly before cutoff, same team, within 732 days, valid integer stats and attempts/targets. Retain exclusions. Do not zero-fill missing appearances. Sources may contain later corrections.
- Use unchanged nfl-workload-residual-v1 stat equations and thresholds: at least 8 training games and 8 earlier rolling errors, last-20 training, last-5 weighting, workload ratio 0.65–1.35, most recent game within 400 days, rolling model MAE strictly below baseline MAE, smoothed last-20 residual estimates, estimated EV >=5% and conditional non-push probability >=55% at actual archived price.
- This diagnostic intentionally cannot evaluate archived injury, roster-freshness and depth-chart gates. Report full live-policy eligibility as UNVERIFIED, not a successful replay or zero-bet performance. Never create fake historical active/starter snapshots or change production gates. Separately label any selected stat/price candidates CONDITIONAL RESEARCH ONLY.
- Select at most one candidate per game/player/market, highest estimated EV; ties sportsbook key, side, then line. Keep all blocked candidates and reasons. Flat one-unit risk per selected candidate; no parlays.
- Persist inputs, model/source hashes and locked selections BEFORE fetching final scoreboards/summaries for grading. Current-season log payloads may contain later rows, but only the strictly pre-cutoff projection is passed to selection. Hash verification prevents changing the selection logic between stages.
- Grade selected candidates with exact ESPN game/player/team IDs, regular-season identity, final status, named box-score statistics and actual line/price. Missing/DNP/unusual status -> REVIEW, never an assumed zero/loss. Full-game props include overtime; no sportsbook participation/promotional rules are assumed. Do not substitute another pick for REVIEW.
- Report W/L/push/review, flat-risk unit return and settled-stake ROI overall and by market, number of distinct games, exclusions and all selected candidates. Multiple props are correlated. Two games cannot establish predictive skill or justify real-money use.
- Store only under snapshots/backtests/2025-12-07-core-props. Never read/write production pick ledgers or merge results into the fresh live record.

Sources: [Odds API historical documentation](https://the-odds-api.com/historical-odds-data/); ESPN public NFL season rosters, game logs and final summaries (exact URLs retained with artifacts).
